Pace Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Thank you for using Pace. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Pace uses, how it is used, and how your information is protected.

Location

Pace may request access to your location to provide workout features such as tracking outdoor workouts, calculating pace and speed, measuring distance travelled, and recording workout routes.

Location information is used only to provide app features. It is not sold, shared, or uploaded to developer servers.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Pace may access Apple Health data, including heart rate, workouts, distance, calories burned, exercise minutes, and other HealthKit data that you choose to allow.

HealthKit data is used only to provide app functionality. It is not used for advertising, analytics, marketing, or profiling, and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Motion & Fitness

Pace may request access to Motion & Fitness data to detect activity, improve workout accuracy, measure movement, and support fitness features.

Motion & Fitness data remains on your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

Data Storage

Pace is designed to keep your information on your device. We do not operate developer servers that store your personal information.

Data Sharing

Pace does not sell your personal information, share it with third parties, track you across other apps or websites, or use your data for targeted advertising.

Your Permissions

You can allow or revoke access to Location, Apple Health, and Motion & Fitness at any time in the Settings app on your device.
